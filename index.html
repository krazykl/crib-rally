A seat at the crib table

Race to 121 points, or choose a quick game to 61. In teams, you and Morgan share one score; Alex and Casey share the other.

  1. Discard: Keep four cards. Two players each discard two; three or four players each discard one. In three-player games, one extra card goes into the crib.
  2. Cut: Tap Cut Deck to reveal the starter. A Jack gives the dealer two points.
  3. Pegging: Tap a card to play. The total cannot exceed 31. Tap Go when no card fits. Fifteens, pairs, runs and 31 score immediately.
  4. Count: Hands count clockwise from the dealer's left, with the dealer's crib last. Tap a scoring category to highlight its combinations. Then Continue.
